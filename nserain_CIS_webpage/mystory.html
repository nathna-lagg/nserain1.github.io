Why Flaaffy?

  • This Pokemon was one of the first Pokemon cards I pulled from a pack back in 2006. The second I saw this goofy sheep standing on its two legs I was hooked.Flaaffy combines soft pink fleece with a glowing striped electric tail that lights up when stored energy peaks. It captures the cozy, cheerful spirit of classic Ken Sugimori designs.
