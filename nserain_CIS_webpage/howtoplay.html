Learn the Trading Card Game

Game Overview & Object of the Game

  • Two players battle their Pokémon in turn-based strategic combat.
  • Knock out opponent's Pokémon to claim Prize cards.
  • Achieve victory by claiming all 6 Prize cards, clearing the opponent's field, or forcing a deck out.

Step-by-Step Play Guide

1

Build Your 60-Card Deck

  • 60 Cards Exactly: Maximum 4 copies of any individual card (except Basic Energy).
  • Pokémon Cards: Basic Pokémon required to start, plus Stage 1 and Stage 2 evolutions.
  • Trainer Cards: Items, Supporters (limited to 1 per turn), and Stadiums.
  • Energy Cards: Provide elemental fuel required to attack and pay retreat costs.
2

Match Setup & Prize Cards

  • Flip a Coin: Decide which player goes first.
  • Shuffle & Draw 7: Both players shuffle decks and draw 7 cards.
  • Place Active & Bench: Place 1 Basic Pokémon in Active Spot, up to 5 on the Bench.
  • Deal 6 Prize Cards: Place 6 cards face-down into your Prize zone.
  • Reveal: Flip Active and Benched Pokémon face-up to begin play.
3

Turn Sequence & Combat

  • 1. Draw Phase: Draw the top card from your deck.
  • 2. Action Phase: Bench Basics, evolve, attach 1 Energy, play Trainers, and retreat.
  • 3. Attack Phase: Choose an attack, apply weakness/resistance, deal damage, and end your turn.

Interactive Playmat Layout

◆ STANDARD BATTLEFIELD PLAYMAT ◆

🏆 Prize Cards

6 cards placed face-down. Take one each time you KO a standard Pokémon.

⚔ Active Spot

Your battling Pokémon. Can attack and take incoming damage.

📚 Draw Deck

60-card deck face-down. Draw 1 card at the start of each turn.

👥 The Bench (Up to 5)

Reserve Pokémon waiting to power up with Energy or evolve into stronger forms.

🗑 Discard Pile

Knocked out Pokémon, used Trainer cards, and spent Energy go here face-up.

Three Ways to Win

Victory Conditions

  • Take all 6 Prize Cards: Claim all 6 Prize cards by knocking out opponent Pokémon.
  • Wipe the Opponent's Field: Knock out all opponent Pokémon so none remain in play.
  • Deck Out: Opponent has no cards left in their deck to draw at the start of their turn.
