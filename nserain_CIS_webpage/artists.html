Artistic Spotlight: Three Iconic Illustrators

While many people look at Pokémon cards purely as gaming pieces or investment assets, card collectors recognize them as miniature art galleries. Since 1996, hundreds of Japanese and international illustrators have contributed their unique perspectives to the Trading Card Game. Below are three of the most influential and celebrated artists in Pokémon history.
